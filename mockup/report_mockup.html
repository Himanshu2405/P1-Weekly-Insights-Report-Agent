            KPI definitions and data notes
            • Weekly Orders Placed: orders created Monday to Sunday (UTC), any status.
            • Weekly Gross Revenue (excl. cancelled): value of items sold in the week, excluding cancelled items. Returns are not subtracted.
            • Weekly Average Order Value: Gross Revenue divided by non-cancelled orders.
            • Weekly Order Cancellation Rate: cancelled orders as a share of orders placed.
            • 14-Day Return Rate: orders the customer returned within 14 days of ordering. Reported only for weeks where every order has had 14 days.
            • Weekly New Customer Signups: new customer accounts created in the week.
            • Targets: 2026 annual plan, same week last year plus 75% growth. Frozen at the start of the year.
            • Source: BigQuery public dataset thelook_ecommerce (a fictional store).
